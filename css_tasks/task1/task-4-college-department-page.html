College Departments Page

CSE

The Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) department focuses on the study of computer systems, software development, programming languages, artificial intelligence, data science, cybersecurity, and cloud computing. Students gain strong theoretical knowledge and practical skills through hands-on projects, internships, and research opportunities, preparing them for successful careers in the rapidly evolving technology industry.

IT

The Information Technology (IT) department equips students with the knowledge and skills required to design, develop, manage, and secure information systems. The curriculum covers networking, database management, web technologies, cloud computing, software engineering, and information security. Students are encouraged to develop innovative solutions that address real-world business and technological challenges.

ECE

The Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE) department provides a comprehensive education in electronic devices, communication systems, embedded systems, signal processing, VLSI design, and wireless technologies. Students gain practical experience through laboratory work, industry projects, and research activities, enabling them to contribute to advancements in telecommunications, automation, and modern electronics.

EEE

The Electrical and Electronics Engineering (EEE) department focuses on electrical power systems, electrical machines, power electronics, renewable energy, control systems, and industrial automation. The program combines theoretical concepts with practical applications to prepare students for careers in power generation, transmission, manufacturing industries, and sustainable energy technologies.

MECHANICAL Engineering

The Mechanical Engineering department provides students with a strong foundation in engineering mechanics, thermodynamics, manufacturing processes, robotics, computer-aided design (CAD), and industrial automation. Through practical laboratory sessions, design projects, and industry exposure, students develop the technical expertise and problem-solving skills needed to excel in automotive, aerospace, energy, manufacturing, and research sectors.
