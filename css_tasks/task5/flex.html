ABOUT

Jackie Chan Adventures is an American animated action-adventure television series inspired by martial arts star Jackie Chan. It originally aired from 2000 to 2005, spanning 5 seasons and 95 episodes.

characters

Heroes Jackie Chan – A skilled martial artist, archaeologist, and agent for Section 13. Jade Chan – Jackie's adventurous niece who often joins him on missions. Uncle – Jackie's wise uncle, an antique shop owner and powerful chi wizard. Tohru – A former member of the Dark Hand who later becomes Uncle's apprentice. Captain Augustus Black – Leader of Section 13 and Jackie's trusted friend. Viper – A skilled cat burglar who later becomes one of Jackie's allies.
