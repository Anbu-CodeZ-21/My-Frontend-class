Engineering College Departments

CSE

Computer Science and Engineering focuses on programming, software development, AI, and computer systems.

IT

Information Technology deals with networking, databases, cybersecurity, and information systems.

ECE

Electronics and Communication Engineering focuses on electronics, communication systems, and embedded technology.

EEE

Electrical and Electronics Engineering studies electrical machines, power systems, and electronics.

Mechanical

Mechanical Engineering deals with machines, manufacturing, thermodynamics, and automobile engineering.
