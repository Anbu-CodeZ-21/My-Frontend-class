IT Company Details

Zoho

Zoho is an Indian multinational technology company that develops cloud-based software for businesses. It provides applications for customer relationship management (CRM), accounting, email, project management, and office productivity. Zoho is known for its innovation, privacy-focused approach, and affordable business solutions.

TCS

Tata Consultancy Services (TCS) is one of the world's largest IT services and consulting companies. It offers software development, cloud computing, artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, and digital transformation services. TCS serves clients across many industries and has offices in numerous countries.

Infosys

Infosys is a leading global IT services and consulting company headquartered in India. It provides services such as software development, business consulting, cloud computing, artificial intelligence, and digital transformation. Infosys is recognized for its strong focus on innovation, technology, and employee development.
