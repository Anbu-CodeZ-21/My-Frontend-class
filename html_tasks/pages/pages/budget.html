BUDGET History

The 2023 Tamil-language action film Leo, starring Thalapathy Vijay and directed by Lokesh Kanagaraj, was made on an estimated budget of ₹250–275 crore. It concluded its successful theatrical run as an all-time blockbuster, amassing a worldwide gross collection of approximately ₹623–624 crore.
